Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your FoldD account and all associated data directly from within the app, at any time — no need to contact support.

How to delete your account (in-app)

  1. Open FoldD and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete My Account.
  3. Confirm by entering your password.
  4. Your account and data are deleted immediately — this cannot be undone.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: name, email, password, and profile (avatar, home city, preferences).
  • Every clothing item, outfit, wishlist entry, packing list, wear log entry, and poll response tied to your account.
  • Your uploaded avatar photo.

This deletion is permanent and immediate — there is no recovery period.

No app access?

If you can't open the app (lost access to your device, forgotten password with no recovery, etc.), email prahlad.singh.dev@gmail.com from the email address on your account and we'll delete it manually, usually within 7 days.
