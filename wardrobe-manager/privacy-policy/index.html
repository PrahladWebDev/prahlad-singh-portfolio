Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This policy explains what information the FoldD app ("FoldD", "we", "our") collects from you, why we collect it, and how it's used. FoldD helps you catalogue your wardrobe, plan outfits, and get weather-based suggestions.

1. Information we collect

CategoryWhat it includes
Account infoName, email address, and password (stored encrypted).
Profile infoOptional avatar photo, home city, and an outfit-rotation preference you can set.
LocationYour approximate or precise location (only if you grant it), used to fetch local weather so we can suggest weather-appropriate outfits.
Wardrobe contentPhotos and details of clothing items you add, outfits you create, wishlist items, packing lists, and your wear-log history.
Poll responsesAnswers you give when using the in-app outfit polls.

2. App permissions

  • Location — used only to fetch local weather for outfit suggestions. You can deny this and still use the app; you just won't get weather-based suggestions.
  • Camera — used when you choose to photograph a clothing item, or scan a barcode/receipt to add an item faster.
  • Photo library — used only when you choose to pick an existing photo for a clothing item or your avatar.
  • Notifications — used for reminders and app updates you can turn off from your device's notification settings.
  • Microphone — declared by a bundled library but not used by any current feature in the app.

Nothing is captured or uploaded from these permissions unless you take an explicit action (tapping to take/pick a photo, etc.).

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, and let you log in.
  • To store and display your wardrobe, outfits, wishlist, and packing lists back to you.
  • To fetch weather for your set or detected location and suggest outfits accordingly.
  • To send account-related emails (email verification).
  • To remind you about rotation of items you haven't worn in a while, if you enable it.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising. Your wardrobe content is private to your account and is not shown to other users.

4. Third-party services

  • Cloudinary — stores and serves photos you upload of clothing items and your avatar.
  • A weather data provider — used only to look up conditions for your set/detected location; your location is not stored by that provider beyond the request.
  • Our own backend server, which stores your account and wardrobe data.

5. Data storage and security

Your login session is stored securely on your device so you stay signed in. Passwords are stored in encrypted (hashed) form — we cannot see your actual password. Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can edit or delete any clothing item, outfit, wishlist entry, or packing list at any time from within the app.
  • You can revoke location, camera, or photo permissions at any time from your device settings.
  • To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us using the details below — we'll confirm once it's done.

7. Children's privacy

FoldD is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact us
