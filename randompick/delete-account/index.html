Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your Random Person Picker account and its associated data at any time. We currently process deletion requests manually to confirm it's really you asking.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to prahlad.singh.dev@gmail.com from the same email address you used to sign up.
  2. Use the subject line: "Random Person Picker account deletion request".
  3. We'll verify the request and delete your account, usually within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: email address and password.
  • Your saved list: every person's name and photo tied to your account.

This deletion is permanent.

Partial deletion

If you don't want to delete your whole account, you can remove individual people (and their photos) from your list at any time directly within the app.
