RANDOM PERSON PICKER

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Random Person Picker is a simple tool for randomly picking a name from a list — for games, chores, giveaways, or anything else you need a fair random pick for.

The short version: Random Person Picker doesn't collect, store, or transmit any of your data anywhere. There are no accounts, no servers, and no internet connection involved in how the app works.

What the app does with your information

  • Names and photos you add for people in your list stay entirely on your own device, held only in the app's memory while it's open. Nothing is uploaded, saved to a server, or shared with us or anyone else.
  • Photo library access is requested only when you choose to add a photo for someone in your list. The app never accesses your photos without you actively picking one, and never uploads them anywhere — the photo is just displayed within the app itself.
  • Closing the app clears this information, since it isn't saved to permanent storage.

Third-party services

None. Random Person Picker doesn't use analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or any other third-party service that would receive data about you or your device.

Children's privacy

Random Person Picker doesn't collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional online feature — this page will be updated first, with a new "Last updated" date.
