RANDOM PERSON PICKER

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Random Person Picker lets you create an account so your list of people (names and photos) is saved and available whenever you sign in. This policy explains what we collect and why.

Information we collect

CategoryWhat it includes
Account infoYour email address and password (stored encrypted, never in plain text).
Your listThe names and, optionally, photos of the people you add to your list.

App permissions

  • Photo library / camera — used only when you choose to attach a photo to someone in your list. Nothing is accessed or uploaded unless you take that action.
  • Contacts — used only if you choose to import people from your phone's contact list. The app reads your contacts' names and photos on your device so you can pick who to add; it does not upload, store, or transmit your full contact list anywhere. Only the specific contacts you select are added to your list (as names/photos), the same as if you'd typed them in by hand.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, and verify your email with a one-time code.
  • To save your list of people and sync it back to you whenever you sign in.
  • To send account-related emails (email verification, password reset codes).

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Third-party services

  • Cloudinary — stores and serves the photos you upload for people in your list, as a processor acting on our behalf. It does not use your photos for its own purposes.
  • Our own backend server, which stores your account and list data.

Data storage and security

Your password is stored in encrypted (hashed) form — we cannot see your actual password. Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Your choices and rights

  • You can edit or remove any person (and their photo) from your list at any time within the app.
  • To delete your account and all associated data, see how to delete your account.

Children's privacy

Random Person Picker's account features are intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact us
