Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your GathaLok account and its associated data at any time. We currently process deletion requests manually to make sure it's really you asking — here's how:

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to prahlad.singh.dev@gmail.com from the same email address you used to sign up for GathaLok.
  2. Use the subject line: "GathaLok account deletion request".
  3. We'll verify the request and delete your account, usually within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, username, email, bio, country, and avatar photo.
  • Your login credentials.
  • Your reading history, bookmarks, achievements, and follow relationships.

What may be retained

  • Stories and comments you've published may be kept on the platform in an anonymized form (no longer linked to your account), since other users may have engaged with or replied to them. Contact us at the email above if you'd like your published stories removed entirely instead of anonymized.
  • We may retain minimal records where required to comply with legal obligations.

Partial deletion

If you don't want to delete your whole account, you can remove individual stories or comments yourself at any time directly within the app, without contacting us.
