GATHALOK

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This policy explains what information the GathaLok app ("GathaLok", "we", "our") collects from you, why we collect it, and how it's used. GathaLok is a folklore and storytelling app where users read, contribute, and discuss stories.

1. Information we collect

CategoryWhat it includes
Account infoName, username, email address, and password (stored encrypted).
Profile infoBio, country, and an optional profile photo (avatar).
Content you createStories you submit, comments, ratings, likes, bookmarks, and who you follow.
Usage infoYour reading history and achievements within the app, used to personalize your experience.
PhotosImages you choose to upload for your avatar or a story's cover image.

We do not access your contacts, precise location, or files unrelated to the features above.

2. App permissions

  • Photo library — requested only when you choose to set an avatar or add a story cover image. Nothing is uploaded unless you pick an image.
  • Microphone — the Android app declares this permission but no current feature in the app uses it; it is not accessed without your action, and this policy will be updated if that changes.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, and let you log in.
  • To show your stories, comments, and profile to other users as intended by the app's social features (follows, likes, leaderboards).
  • To send account-related emails: email verification and password reset links.
  • To operate features like bookmarks, reading history, and achievements.
  • To moderate content (for example, reviewing reported stories or comments).

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Third-party services

GathaLok uses the following outside services to operate the app:

  • Cloudinary — stores and serves uploaded images (avatars, story covers).
  • Our own backend server, which stores account and content data.

These providers process data only as needed to provide their service to us and do not use your data for their own purposes.

5. Data storage and security

Your login session is stored securely on your device so you stay signed in. Passwords are stored in encrypted (hashed) form — we cannot see your actual password. Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can edit your profile, bio, avatar, and password at any time from Settings.
  • You can delete stories, comments, or bookmarks you've created.
  • To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us using the details below — we'll confirm once it's done.

7. Children's privacy

GathaLok is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach out at prahlad.singh.dev@gmail.com.
